Weather Beat

Pick a time, a sky and a city. The page turns them into hand percussion, a modular-style synth line and a picture. Same conditions always give the same beat.

Delay on each track

Switch a track's delay on, then turn Time and Feedback while the beat plays.

Use your own recordings

Everything you hear is built from scratch in the browser. Load a recorded hit for any drum voice and it replaces the synthesized one, with small pitch and timing variation on every strike.
